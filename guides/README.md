## Universal mechanics (read this first)

These rules apply to **every** faction. The individual guides build on them.

### The core rule: home economies are capped — TERRITORY is the economy
Every faction's home/base income **plateaus at a low ceiling** — enough to defend and
keep a modest army, never enough to win. Real scaling comes from **holding contested
ground on the map**:

- **Obelisks** — neutral capture points. Stand units nearby (uncontested) for ~6s to
  claim one; while held it pays a steady **+3.4 resource/sec**. These are the single
  biggest, uncapped income source in the game. Whoever holds the most Obelisks usually wins.
- **Wellsprings** — indestructible fonts. You can't capture them by standing on them;
  you **harness** one by raising your faction's designated economy building within ~215px
  of it (the Myriad instead blankets it in creep). A harnessed font pours out ~4.2–5.5 of
  your primary resource per second. Some factions get bonus effects (Verdant gets Pollen +
  Loam + an ecosystem buff; Stormforge fonts *ramp* the longer you hold them).
- **Hoards / Caches / Munitions Bunkers** — guarded neutral treasure. Destroy them for a
  one-time bounty (Cache +160, Bunker +450, Hoard +550). Bunkers also drop 90 Powder to a Warden.

> **Two exceptions:** the **Warden** is self-sufficient (income scales with its buildings'
> total HP) and *cannot* harness Wellsprings. The **Exodus** has no base and chases finite
> crystal nodes around the map instead.

### Building placement: the network rule
Most factions must place each new building **within connection range (~270px)** of one they
already own, so a base grows as a connected web. To reach distant nodes/Obelisks you must
physically *chain* buildings out toward them. Exceptions: Myriad builds on creep, Choir builds
within its lattice, Exodus has no buildings, and the Syndicate **Watchpost air-drops anywhere**.
You can never build within ~300px of an enemy structure (no turret-rushing their base).

### Research: the shared four-branch tech tree
Every faction researches at its own lab building. The tree is identical in structure — four
branches, each with four tiers:

- **Offense:** Weapons I/II (+20%/+20% dmg) → Targeting Array (+18% fire rate) → **Siege Ordnance** (+40% splash, +10% dmg)
- **Defense:** Plating I/II (+18%/+20% HP) → Ward Fields (+30% shields) → Fortification (+22% HP)
- **Mobility:** Engines I/II (+15%/+15% speed) → Long Optics (+18% range) → Overdrive (+12% speed, +8% fire rate)
- **Economy:** Logistics I/II (+15%/+18% income) → Supply Lines (+12 unit cap) → War Economy (+20% income, +8 cap)

**Siege Ordnance** (the Offense capstone) is the prerequisite for every faction's **apex unit**.

### Apex tech
Each faction has a late-game **super-structure + titan** gated behind deep tech *and* Siege
Ordnance — ruinously expensive, the payoff for teching all game. (The Warden instead has its
own apex line: Castellan → The Bulwark → The Worldbreaker.)

### Winning
Free-for-all, last core standing wins. Destroy every enemy **core** (HQ / Hive / Ark / Ossuary /
Haven / Keep / Pyre / Heartwood / Reactor / Altar); protect your own. Lose your core and you're out.
