# Solari Exodus — Faction Guide

> A pilgrimage with no homeland. You have **no base and never will** — one mobile **Ark** is
> your fortress, factory and treasury in one. Every warrior is shielded and priceless.

- **Core:** The Ark (2300 HP / 900 shield at tier 0) — mobile, builds everything, can deploy to siphon
- **Resource:** Energy / Crystal
- **Base unit cap:** 28 (the lowest — your units are elite, not numerous)
- **Identity:** A roaming death-ball. Shields regenerate out of combat, so you **strike and fall
  back**. If the Ark dies, you lose instantly.

---

## 1. Economy — Collectors + Ark siphon, chasing finite nodes

You have no buildings, so no passive economy. Two income streams:
- **Collectors** (60) — harvesters that mine crystal nodes and haul it back to the Ark. Build
  more to scale. Like the Vanguard, **nodes are finite** — keep the Ark and Collectors moving to
  fresh nodes.
- **Ark siphon** — move the Ark onto a crystal node and **Deploy** it to siphon Energy fast
  (+6/s). Deployed, the Ark is stationary but pulls hard.
- **Wellsprings (NEW)** — the nomads now **harness fonts**: park **Collectors or the Ark** within
  range of a Wellspring to claim it for **+6/s** (uncapped). Sending a couple of idle Collectors
  to sit on fonts across the map is now a major, scalable income stream — your reward for ranging.
- **Obelisks** — capture with your units for **+5/s each** (Exodus earns a bonus on top of the
  standard rate). With your low cap, every Obelisk matters a lot — grab and hold them.

**Scale by exploring the map.** Between finite nodes, harnessed fonts and Obelisks, your whole
economy is now about spreading Collectors and the Ark across contested ground and holding it.

## 2. The Ark ascension ladder — your tech tree AND your superweapon

The Ark is a **heavy gunship from tier 1**: a **splashing main beam** plus a ring of **four
point-defence guns** — so even a fresh Ark hits groups hard and defends itself, no longer a
soft target. Select the Ark and **Upgrade** it through **eight tiers**. Each makes it
dramatically bigger, tougher and deadlier — size, HP, shields, beam damage, **splash radius**,
range, **gun count (4 → 8)** *and* the Solar Lance all scale — and it visibly grows from a
cathedral-fortress into a literal **walking sun**. The first three tiers are affordable power
spikes; the last four are a long, ruinously expensive climb that ends in a **roaming superweapon
surpassing the Warden's Worldbreaker** (a fully-ascended Ark sustains ~700 DPS with huge splash,
an eight-gun ring and 25,000 HP, on top of the Solar Lance).

| Tier | Upgrade | Cost | HP / Shield | ×stats |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reinforced Ark | 600 | 3400 / 1400 | ×1.3 |
| 2 | Radiant Ark | 1500 | 4900 / 2100 | ×1.65 |
| 3 | Ascendant Ark | 3200 | 6800 / 3000 | ×2.05 |
| 4 | Empyrean Ark | 5500 | 9500 / 4200 | ×2.6 |
| 5 | Sunforged Ark | 8500 | 13000 / 5800 | ×3.3 |
| 6 | Dawnbringer Ark | 13000 | 18000 / 8000 | ×4.2 |
| 7 | **Solar Apotheosis** | **20000** | **25000 / 11000** | **×5.2** |

**Cumulative cost to fully ascend: ~52,300 Energy** — a genuine win-the-game investment.
At the apex the Ark has **25,000 HP** (the Worldbreaker has 9,000), a beam that out-DPSes
and out-ranges the Worldbreaker's cannon, and a **Solar Lance** (see below) that hits harder,
splashes wider and reloads faster than the Worldbreaker's Gustav Strike. Reaching it is hard
and slow by design — but a Solar Apotheosis Ark is, unit-for-unit, the strongest thing in the game.

Upgrading the Ark is usually better than more units (your cap is only 28). Take tiers 1–3 as
soon as you can afford them; treat tiers 4–7 as your late-game victory project, funded by deep
map control (Obelisks + siphon).

## 2b. Active ability — the Solar Lance

The Ark can fire a **Solar Lance**: a telegraphed orbital beam that scorches a wide blast at any
point in great range (2600), on a 46s reload. Target it from the Ark's command card (left-click
the impact point; Esc/right-click cancels). **Its damage and blast scale with the Ark's tier** —
modest at tier 0 (175 dmg), apocalyptic at the apex (~910 dmg / 322 splash, out-doing the
Worldbreaker's nuke). Fire it on cooldown: into clumps, onto turtled bases, or to soften a core
before you walk in. A telegraph warns the enemy, so aim where they can't easily scatter.

## 3. Units (all built from the Ark)

| Unit | Cost | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Collector** | 60 | Harvester. Build several early. |
| **Seeker** | 120 | Cheap shielded skirmisher that **blinks** onto its target. |
| **Lancer** | 220 | Long-range beam (235). Fragile, hits hard — your damage core. |
| **Guardian** | 180 | Shield-and-heal **aura** support. Keeps the ball alive. |
| **Phoenix** | 150 | Fast shielded flyer, rapid fire. Raids and chases. |
| **Templar** | 260 | Shielded splashing siege shell. |
| **Aegis** | 300 | 240 HP / 180 shield bruiser — soaks fire up front. |
| **Solar Frigate** | 480 | 540 HP / 360 shield — a towering second capital ship. Broadside batteries wheel to bear on a target but keep firing on the move once aligned, rather than stopping dead. Slow to turn; escort it. |

**Apex:** **Solar Sovereign** (820) — shielded capital ship with a splashing beam + four
point-defence guns. Built straight from the Ark (no super-structure needed), gated on Siege Ordnance.

**The death-ball:** Aegis front → Lancers/Templars behind → Guardian(s) layering shields →
Seekers/Phoenixes flanking. Pull back when shields drop; they regenerate and you re-engage at full.

---

## 4. How to play
- **Never overcommit.** Shields regen out of combat — every fight should end with you retreating
  to recharge, then returning. Attrition is your weapon.
- **Keep the Ark safe but useful.** It's your best unit *and* your loss condition. Deploy it to
  siphon when safe; bring it to fights as an anchor when winning; never let it get surrounded.
- **Upgrade the Ark** whenever you can afford it — it scales harder than unit count given your low cap.
- Grab Obelisks; every point of uncapped income matters when you field so few bodies.

---

## 5. Strengths, weaknesses, win conditions
**Strengths:** elite, shield-regenerating units; an unkillable-feeling upgraded Ark; total mobility
(relocate your whole "base" instantly).
**Weaknesses:** lowest cap; no static defense; **single point of failure** (Ark death = loss);
fragile if you fight without retreating to recharge shields.
**Win conditions:** roam as an unbeatable death-ball, win every fight on shield-attrition, upgrade
the Ark to a tier the enemy can't crack, then walk it into their core. Keep the Ark alive above all else.
