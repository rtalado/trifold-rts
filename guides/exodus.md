# Solari Exodus — Faction Guide

> A pilgrimage with no homeland. You have **no base and never will** — one mobile **Ark** is
> your fortress, factory and treasury in one. Every warrior is shielded and priceless.

- **Core:** The Ark (2300 HP / 900 shield at tier 0) — mobile, builds everything, can deploy to siphon
- **Resource:** Energy / Crystal
- **Base unit cap:** 28 (the lowest — your units are elite, not numerous)
- **Identity:** A roaming death-ball. Shields regenerate out of combat, so you **strike and fall
  back**. If the Ark dies, you lose instantly.

---

## 1. Economy — Collectors + Ark siphon, chasing finite nodes

You have no buildings, so no passive economy. Two income streams:
- **Collectors** (60) — harvesters that mine crystal nodes and haul it back to the Ark. Build
  more to scale. Like the Vanguard, **nodes are finite** — keep the Ark and Collectors moving to
  fresh nodes.
- **Ark siphon** — move the Ark onto a crystal node and **Deploy** it to siphon Energy fast
  (+4.5/s). Deployed, the Ark is stationary but pulls hard.

The Exodus *cannot* harness Wellsprings (it already chases nodes) but **can** capture Obelisks
with its units for +3.4/s — do it; the low cap means each Obelisk matters a lot.

---

## 2. The Ark upgrade ladder — your tech tree

Select the Ark and **Upgrade** it through four tiers. Each makes it dramatically bigger,
tougher and deadlier (and it visibly grows into a walking cathedral-fortress):

| Tier | Upgrade | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reinforced Ark | 600 | 3400 HP / 1400 shield, ×1.3 stats |
| 2 | Radiant Ark | 1500 | 4900 HP / 2100 shield, ×1.65 |
| 3 | Ascendant Ark | 3200 | 6800 HP / 3000 shield, ×2.05 |

A maxed Ark is a monster that can solo whole armies. Upgrading it is often better than more units.

## 3. Units (all built from the Ark)

| Unit | Cost | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Collector** | 60 | Harvester. Build several early. |
| **Seeker** | 120 | Cheap shielded skirmisher that **blinks** onto its target. |
| **Lancer** | 220 | Long-range beam (235). Fragile, hits hard — your damage core. |
| **Guardian** | 180 | Shield-and-heal **aura** support. Keeps the ball alive. |
| **Phoenix** | 150 | Fast shielded flyer, rapid fire. Raids and chases. |
| **Templar** | 260 | Shielded splashing siege shell. |
| **Aegis** | 300 | 240 HP / 180 shield bruiser — soaks fire up front. |

**Apex:** **Solar Sovereign** (820) — shielded capital ship with a splashing beam + four
point-defence guns. Built straight from the Ark (no super-structure needed), gated on Siege Ordnance.

**The death-ball:** Aegis front → Lancers/Templars behind → Guardian(s) layering shields →
Seekers/Phoenixes flanking. Pull back when shields drop; they regenerate and you re-engage at full.

---

## 4. How to play
- **Never overcommit.** Shields regen out of combat — every fight should end with you retreating
  to recharge, then returning. Attrition is your weapon.
- **Keep the Ark safe but useful.** It's your best unit *and* your loss condition. Deploy it to
  siphon when safe; bring it to fights as an anchor when winning; never let it get surrounded.
- **Upgrade the Ark** whenever you can afford it — it scales harder than unit count given your low cap.
- Grab Obelisks; every point of uncapped income matters when you field so few bodies.

---

## 5. Strengths, weaknesses, win conditions
**Strengths:** elite, shield-regenerating units; an unkillable-feeling upgraded Ark; total mobility
(relocate your whole "base" instantly).
**Weaknesses:** lowest cap; no static defense; **single point of failure** (Ark death = loss);
fragile if you fight without retreating to recharge shields.
**Win conditions:** roam as an unbeatable death-ball, win every fight on shield-attrition, upgrade
the Ark to a tier the enemy can't crack, then walk it into their core. Keep the Ark alive above all else.
