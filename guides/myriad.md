# Myriad Swarm — Faction Guide

> A living tide. Your **creep IS your economy**, your buildings spawn an **endless free swarm**,
> and you win by drowning the map in bodies.

- **Core:** Hive (2100 HP) — spreads creep, spawns free Drones, grows every structure
- **Resource:** Biomass (from creep-covered tiles)
- **Base unit cap:** 90 (by far the highest — you are meant to flood)
- **Identity:** Free, endlessly-respawning units + territory you literally paint onto the map.

---

## 1. Economy — creep tiles, but the home blob caps out

You earn Biomass per **creep tile** you cover: `2.0 + 0.012 × tiles` — but the tile bonus
**plateaus at 480 tiles**. A home creep-blob maxes out fast. To keep growing you must creep
**outward** and, above all, **blanket Wellsprings**:

- The Myriad harnesses a Wellspring simply by **covering it in creep** (no building needed) —
  each font pours out ~5.2 Biomass/s, far more than tiles ever will.
- Capture **Obelisks** with your swarm for +3.4/s.
- **Creep Tumors** are cheap (50) spreaders — chain them toward fonts, Obelisks and chokes.
  They also extend where you can build.

---

## 2. The free swarm — your whole army can be free

These breeding structures spawn units **forever, for free**, on a timer:
- **Spawn Pit** → Drones (cheap melee chaff)
- **Spitter Mound** → Spitters (ranged acid) — needs a Spawn Pit
- **Hunter Den** → Hunters (fast melee runners) — needs a Spawn Pit

Stack several of each and you get a self-replenishing army at zero ongoing cost. Your Biomass
then goes into **elites** and tech instead of basic bodies.

**Paid elites (from the Hive):** Broodmother (300, splash bruiser, needs Hunter Den) · Ravager
(200, ranged corrosive splash, needs Hunter Den). **Defense:** Acid Spine (120 static turret).

**Units heal on creep** — fights on your own creep are massively in your favour. Extend creep
toward the enemy before committing.

**Apex:** **Brood Tyrant** (650) from the **Brood Nexus** — a colossal brood-mother that crushes
and **heals the swarm around it**. Needs Hunter Den + Evolution Chamber + Siege Ordnance.

---

## 3. Controls & build order
- Select the **Hive** and **right-click** to set the swarm **rally point** — where free spawns gather.
1. Hive → spread initial creep; make a few **Tumors** toward your nearest expansion.
2. **Spawn Pit** ASAP (free Drones start flowing).
3. Creep toward and **cover a Wellspring** — your economy takes off.
4. **Spitter Mound + Hunter Den** for a free ranged+fast swarm.
5. **Evolution Chamber** for upgrades; **Acid Spines** on chokes.
6. Keep tumoring outward; flood elites + Tyrant late.

---

## 4. Tech priorities (Evolution Chamber)
- **Offense → Weapons** makes your huge unit count terrifying (it multiplies across the swarm).
- **Economy → Logistics** to compound Biomass.
- **Mobility → Engines** so the swarm arrives together and runs down fleeing foes.
- **Siege Ordnance** for Ravager/Tyrant splash.

---

## 5. Strengths, weaknesses, win conditions
**Strengths:** enormous cap; free regenerating army; heals on creep; cheap, fast map control.
**Weaknesses:** individual units are weak — you rely on numbers and creep; if pushed off your
creep into the open you're at a disadvantage; the home economy caps, so passive play stalls.
**Win conditions:** blanket the map in creep (denying it to others while harnessing every font),
keep free swarm streaming from many breeding structures, and overwhelm cores with sheer body
count under Ravager/Tyrant support. Protect the Hive — and keep creep alive.
