# Myriad Swarm — Faction Guide

> A living tide. Your **creep IS your economy**, your buildings spawn an **endless free swarm**,
> and you win by drowning the map in bodies.

- **Core:** Hive (2100 HP) — spreads creep, spawns free Drones, grows every structure
- **Resource:** Biomass (from creep-covered tiles)
- **Base unit cap:** 90 (by far the highest — you are meant to flood)
- **Identity:** Free, endlessly-respawning units + territory you literally paint onto the map.

---

## 1. Economy — creep tiles, but the home blob caps out

You earn Biomass per **creep tile** you cover: `2.0 + 0.012 × tiles` — but the tile bonus
**plateaus at 480 tiles**. A home creep-blob maxes out fast. To keep growing you must creep
**outward** and, above all, **blanket Wellsprings**:

- The Myriad harnesses a Wellspring simply by **covering it in creep** (no building needed) —
  each font pours out ~5.2 Biomass/s, far more than tiles ever will.
- Capture **Obelisks** with your swarm for +3.4/s.
- **Creep Tumors** are cheap (50) spreaders — chain them toward fonts, Obelisks and chokes.
  They also extend where you can build.

---

## 2. The free swarm — your whole army can be free

These breeding structures spawn units **forever, for free**, on a timer:
- **Spawn Pit** → Drones (cheap melee chaff)
- **Spitter Mound** → Spitters (ranged acid) — needs a Spawn Pit
- **Hunter Den** → Hunters (fast melee runners) — needs a Spawn Pit

Stack several of each and you get a self-replenishing army at zero ongoing cost. Your Biomass
then goes into **elites** and tech instead of basic bodies.

**Paid elites (from the Hive):** Broodmother (300, splash bruiser, needs Hunter Den) · Ravager
(200, ranged corrosive splash, needs Hunter Den). **Defense:** Acid Spine (120 static turret).

**Units heal on creep** — fights on your own creep are massively in your favour. Extend creep
toward the enemy before committing.

**Apex:** **Brood Tyrant** (650) from the **Brood Nexus** — a colossal brood-mother that crushes
and **heals the swarm around it**. Needs Hunter Den + Evolution Chamber + Siege Ordnance.

---

## 3. Corruption — the swarm rots everything it touches

**Every Myriad attack corrupts the enemy it hits.** A corrupted foe:
- **deals ~20% less damage** (your fragile bodies survive longer),
- **moves ~15% slower**, and
- **slowly rots** (a corrosion damage-over-time).

A normal hit corrupts for ~3s; **corruption units/towers** apply a much longer dose (6–8s) so the
debuff sticks even as they kite. The signature payoff: **when a corrupted enemy *unit* dies, it
bursts free Larva from its corpse** — the swarm literally erupts from the dead. A big corrupted
fight snowballs into a tide of fresh bodies (bounded by your Larva cap). Corrupted enemies show a
pulsing violet aura.

### The corruption wing (buildings)
- **Infestation Pit** (160) — breeds **free Larva** endlessly (separate Larva cap, like a second free army).
- **Corruption Den** (220, needs Evolution Chamber) — breeds the corrosive elites below.
- **Miasma Vent** (140) — a static tower whose acid bolts **heavily corrupt** attackers at your creep edge.

### Corruption units
| Unit | Cost | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Larva** | free | Tiny, very fast melee chaff. Bred by the Infestation Pit and burst from corrupted corpses. |
| **Corruptor** | 170 | Ranged spore-caster; long, heavy corruption — wither the enemy army before it arrives. |
| **Defiler** | 260 | Heavy bile-beast: long-range corrosive **splash** + deep corruption. Your siege / turtle-breaker. |
| **Mawflyer** | 160 | Fast **flying** acid-spitter — the swarm's air; raids, chases flyers, corrupts on bite. |

### Active ability — Corruption Bloom (the Hive)
Target a point in great range (2600) to erupt a spore-bloom that **heavily corrupts every enemy in
a wide radius** (9s) and deals a burst of corrosion (~40s reload). Drop it on a clumped army before
you engage: they hit softer, rot, and any that die burst into Larva for you. A fight-winning button.

---

## 4. Controls & build order
- Select the **Hive** and **right-click** to set the swarm **rally point** — where free spawns gather.
1. Hive → spread initial creep; make a few **Tumors** toward your nearest expansion.
2. **Spawn Pit** ASAP (free Drones start flowing); add an **Infestation Pit** for free Larva.
3. Creep toward and **cover a Wellspring** — your economy takes off.
4. **Spitter Mound + Hunter Den** for a free ranged+fast swarm.
5. **Evolution Chamber** → **Corruption Den** to open Corruptors/Defilers/Mawflyers.
6. **Acid Spines / Miasma Vents** on chokes; keep tumoring outward; flood elites + Tyrant late.
7. Open every big fight with **Corruption Bloom**, then bury them under bodies + burst Larva.

---

## 5. Tech priorities (Evolution Chamber)
- **Offense → Weapons** makes your huge unit count terrifying (it multiplies across the swarm).
- **Economy → Logistics** to compound Biomass.
- **Mobility → Engines** so the swarm arrives together and runs down fleeing foes.
- **Siege Ordnance** for Ravager/Defiler/Tyrant splash.

---

## 6. Strengths, weaknesses, win conditions
**Strengths:** enormous cap; free regenerating army (now two — main swarm + Larva); heals on creep;
cheap, fast map control; **corruption** that weakens every enemy you fight and turns their dead into
*your* reinforcements.
**Weaknesses:** individual units are weak — you rely on numbers, creep and corruption; if pushed off
your creep into the open you're at a disadvantage; the home economy caps, so passive play stalls.
**Win conditions:** blanket the map in creep (harnessing every font), stream free swarm + Larva from
many breeding structures, and grind the enemy down with **corruption** — Bloom their army, let it rot
and burst into Larva, and overwhelm cores under Defiler/Tyrant support. Protect the Hive — and keep creep alive.
