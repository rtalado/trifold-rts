# Obsidian Pact — Faction Guide

> Death is your harvest — but **only your own**. Every unit of yours that falls spills **Blood**
> to fund the next, greater summoning. Reckless by design: feed the grinder.

- **Core:** Blood Altar (1800 HP) — summons Thralls, Zealots, Behemoths
- **Resource:** Blood (from your OWN units dying)
- **Base unit cap:** 72 (high — you're meant to throw bodies away)
- **Identity:** Martyrdom. Cheap expendable Thralls die → Blood → bigger horrors. Sacrifice fuels escalation — and **every death enrages the survivors**.

---

## 1. Economy — your dead pay you

There is **no passive home income** to speak of. You gain Blood when **your own units die**:
`6 flat + 10% of their max HP` per death. So the loop is deliberately morbid:

> Summon cheap **Thralls** (30 each) → throw them into the fight → they die → Blood → summon
> **Behemoths** (340) from the proceeds.

- **Bone Shrines** spawn free Thralls forever — an endless supply of expendable bodies (and Blood
  when they fall). Stack them.
- Raise **Bone Shrines beside Wellsprings** to bleed extra Blood (~5/s) from the map.
- Capture **Obelisks** for +3.4/s.

The Pact wants to be **constantly fighting and losing chaff** — a Pact whose units survive idle
isn't earning. Trade aggressively; every death (yours) is income.

---

## 2. Blood Frenzy — your dying makes the rest deadlier

When a Pact unit dies, **every Pact unit near it flies into a frenzy** for a few seconds — striking
**~30% harder and ~28% faster** (a hot crimson aura marks the enraged). Because you're *already*
throwing bodies into the grinder, the horde is in a near-permanent rolling frenzy in any real fight:
the front rank dies, the rank behind it goes berserk, and so on. Martyrdom in motion.

- **Keep your chaff packed in** so a death frenzies as many bodies as possible.
- The Altar's **Crimson Rite** (active) ruptures a field of enemies for burst damage **and frenzies
  every nearby Pact unit at once** — pop it as the lines clash. ~40s reload.

## 3. Units

| Unit | Cost | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Thrall** | 30 | Dirt-cheap expendable melee body. Its death spills Blood + frenzies. Spam these. |
| **Flayer** | 80 | Fast frenzied striker — flings itself into the grinder. |
| **Zealot** | 110 | Tougher melee fanatic (needs Shrine). |
| **Behemoth** | 340 | Massive splashing horror raised from spilled Blood (needs Shrine). |
| **Cultist** | 70 | Cheap ranged hex-spitter. |
| **Blood Priest** | 130 | **Healer** — bleeds its life into a horde-mending aura (Flesh Vats). |
| **Abomination** | 300 | Heavy splashing bruiser, cheap to mass (Flesh Vats). |
| **Gargoyle** | 150 | Winged blood-fiend — the Pact's **air**; lashes from afar (Flesh Vats). |

**Buildings:** Bone Shrine (120, free Thralls) · Blood Spike (110, cheap turret) · **Hemorrhage
Spire** (160, heavier splashing wall-gun) · **Flesh Vats** (200, raises the elite horrors — needs the
Blood Sanctum) · Blood Sanctum (research lab).

**Apex:** **Blood Avatar** (600) from the **Grand Altar** — an avatar of slaughter: splashing
strikes, a horde-healing aura, and an **endless Thrall tide**. Needs Sanctum + Shrine + Siege Ordnance.

---

## 4. Build order
1. Altar → **Thralls** en masse; **Bone Shrine** ASAP for the free Thrall stream.
2. Throw Thralls / **Flayers** at the enemy / neutrals early — pressure, Blood, and rolling frenzy.
3. Add **Cultists** for ranged support; **Zealots** to stiffen the line.
4. **Blood Sanctum** → **Flesh Vats**: a **Blood Priest** (your only healer) keeps the horde alive,
   **Abominations** form the heavy line, **Gargoyles** give you air.
5. More Shrines (free Thralls + Blood); Shrines on Wellsprings for extra Blood.
6. **Crimson Rite** as the lines meet; Grand Altar + Blood Avatar to headline the swarm late.

---

## 5. Tech priorities (Blood Sanctum)
- **Offense → Weapons** so your horde and Behemoths hit hard.
- **Defense → Plating** to keep Behemoths grinding (and Thralls live *just* long enough to matter).
- **Economy → Logistics** to compound Blood.
- **Siege Ordnance** for Behemoth/Avatar splash.

---

## 6. Strengths, weaknesses, win conditions
**Strengths:** endless cheap bodies; high cap; economy that *rewards* losing chaff (you can trade
forever); **blood frenzy makes a dying horde hit far above its cost**; now with a healer (Blood
Priest), air (Gargoyle) and a cheap heavy (Abomination); strong escalation into Behemoths/Avatar.
**Weaknesses:** mostly melee — vulnerable to kiting and splash; relies on bodies dying to fund
itself, so a stalemate where nothing dies starves you; little without a fight to feed it.
**Win conditions:** feed the grinder — keep a Thrall tide dying into the enemy while the Blood it
spills raises Behemoths and a Blood Avatar, then bury their core under the horde. Keep the Altar.
