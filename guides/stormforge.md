# Stormforge Dynasty — Faction Guide

> An engine that **accelerates**. Few machines, but heavily **shielded** and devastating — and
> your power output ramps the longer you hold the map.

- **Core:** Storm Reactor (2000 HP) — assembles machines
- **Resource:** Power
- **Base unit cap:** 42 (low — your units are big, shielded and expensive)
- **Identity:** Snowball through escalating industry + shield-sustained pushes. Seize ground
  early and never let go.

---

## 1. Economy — flat at home, accelerating on the map

- **Dynamos** pay a **flat** rate and **plateau at 7** (`2.4 + 1.4 per Dynamo`). Idling at home no
  longer scales you — the acceleration moved onto the map.
- **Storm Fonts (Wellsprings):** raise a **Dynamo beside a Wellspring** to harness it — and the
  payout **ramps the longer you hold it** (`× (1 + time × 0.0022)`). A font held since minute 2 is
  worth far more at minute 15. **Seize fonts early and defend them to the death.**
- Capture **Obelisks** for +3.4/s.

The Stormforge identity: early map control compounds into a late-game power advantage nobody
else can match — *if* you hold your ground.

---

## 2. Shields & the Charge Pylon

Your machines have **shields** (regenerate out of combat) on top of HP. The key building:
- **Charge Pylon** — projects a field that **re-energises shields** (and slowly hull) of nearby
  machines mid-fight. A push escorted by Pylons **never runs out of shield** — it just keeps
  grinding. Build Pylons into your army's wake as you advance.

---

## 3. Units

| Unit | Cost | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Arclight** | 110 | Fast shielded skirmisher, rapid fire. |
| **Galvan** | 180 | Fast, heavily-shielded shock-trooper (120 shield); short-range splash. Front line. |
| **Voltaic** | 210 | Long-range shielded beam platform (230). Damage core. |
| **Gladius** | 230 | Shielded mid-weight mech, splashing cannon. |
| **Colossus** | 420 | Huge shielded walker, splashing siege cannon (from the **Foundry**). |
| **Storm Cruiser** | 500 | 600 HP / 400 shield — a towering broadside battlecruiser (from the **Foundry**). Wheels to bring its arc-cannons to bear and keeps firing on the move once aligned, rather than stopping dead. |

**Defense:** Tesla Coil (beam tower). **Research:** Research Bay.

**Apex:** **Storm Titan** (780) from the **Grand Foundry** — long arc-beam + four arc turrets
behind heavy shields (1100 HP / 500 shield). Needs Foundry + Research Bay + Siege Ordnance.

**Composition:** Galvans front (soaking on shields) → Voltaics/Gladius/Colossus behind →
Pylons trailing to keep everyone charged. A slow, shielded, unkillable grind.

---

## 4. Build order
1. Reactor → **Arclights** for safety + scouting.
2. **Dynamos** (toward the cap of 6) to get Power flowing.
3. Rush a **Dynamo onto a Wellspring** early — start the ramp ASAP, it pays off all game.
4. **Charge Pylons** as you build an army; **Research Bay** for tech.
5. Galvan/Voltaic core; **Foundry** for Colossus.
6. Hold every font, expand pylon-backed pushes, Storm Titan to finish.

---

## 5. Tech priorities (Research Bay)
- **Defense → Ward Fields (+30% shields)** is gold for you — it scales your entire shielded army
  and the Pylon sustain.
- **Offense → Weapons** for your low-count, high-value machines.
- **Economy → Logistics** compounds with the ramping fonts.
- **Siege Ordnance** for Colossus/Titan splash.

---

## 6. Strengths, weaknesses, win conditions
**Strengths:** shield sustain (Pylons make pushes unstoppable); a late game that accelerates past
everyone if you hold fonts; tough, high-value machines.
**Weaknesses:** low cap and expensive units (you can't replace losses quickly); flat/capped home
economy punishes passivity; weak if you lose your early fonts (the ramp resets with them); a
rough early game before shields and Pylons come online.
**Win conditions:** grab Storm Fonts early and defend them so the ramp compounds, then roll a
Pylon-charged shield-ball over everything. The longer the game and the more ground you've held,
the more you out-power the field. Defend the Reactor.
