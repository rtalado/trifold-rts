# Gilded Syndicate — Faction Guide

> *"Gold breeds gold."* A mercantile cartel that wages war with money: instant-hire
> mercenaries, kill bounties, severance insurance, air-dropped infrastructure, and a
> treasury that compounds.

- **Core:** The Haven (1700 HP, fires a sidearm, treasury + main hire hall)
- **Resource:** Gold
- **Base unit cap:** 40
- **Identity:** No production queues to speak of — mercs arrive **instantly** for a price.
  You win by banking gold so it compounds, then converting the overflow into an army that
  materialises the moment you can afford it.

---

## 1. How the economy works

Your treasury earns **compound interest** every second: `gain ≈ 2.5 + 0.011 × min(treasury, cap)`.
The more gold you're sitting on, the faster it grows — but only up to the **interest cap**.

**The cap is driven by TERRITORY, not by buildings:**

| Source | Cap added | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Base | 700 | a bank in a corner stalls here |
| Each **Countinghouse** | +150 | also pays +0.4/s flat rent |
| Each **Bullion Vault** | +500 | also pays +1.0/s flat rent — the heavy treasury piece |
| Each held **Obelisk / harnessed Wellspring** | **+650** | the real lever |

So a Syndicate that never leaves home plateaus around a 700-cap trickle. A Syndicate holding
four Obelisks has a cap near 3300+ and a fortune compounding on top of it. **Get on the map.**

**Other income:**
- **Kill bounty** — every enemy unit/building you kill pays `10 + 6% of its max HP`. Aggression literally pays.
- **Severance insurance (NEW)** — when one of *your* mercs dies, **25% of its gold cost is
  refunded** to the treasury. This smooths the instant-hire churn: trading armies is far less
  punishing for you than for anyone else. Lean into it — re-hire and re-engage.
- **Obelisks** — +3.4/s each (on top of the +650 cap they grant). **Wellsprings** — +5.5/s
  (harness by dropping a **Watchpost** beside one).

### Economy golden rules
1. **Don't hoard gold you can't earn interest on.** Spending below your cap costs you almost
   nothing in interest; spending the part *above* the cap is "free" overflow — that's your army budget.
2. **Bank to the cap, spend the overflow.** Build economy/territory first so the cap is high,
   *then* the compounding does the work and you buy mercs with the surplus.
3. **Territory > buildings** for the cap. Two Obelisks beat any number of Countinghouses.

## 2. Buildings

| Building | Cost | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **The Haven** (core) | — | Treasury; hires Enforcer/Arbalest/Juggernaut/Marauder; **Reinforcement Drop** ability; light gun |
| **Watchpost** | 140 | Cheap forward gun — **air-drops anywhere** (not into enemy territory). Harnesses Wellsprings. |
| **Gun Bastion** (NEW) | 220 | Tanky long-range splashing turret (900 HP). The defensive backbone of a hold. Must connect to base. |
| **Countinghouse** | 200 | +150 interest cap, +0.4/s rent |
| **Bullion Vault** (NEW) | 300 | +500 interest cap, +1.0/s rent — the heavy treasury upgrade |
| **Mercenary Guild** (NEW) | 200 | Hires the specialist mercs (see below) |
| **Black Market** | 150 | Research lab (the shared four-branch tech tree) |
| **War Exchange** (apex) | 500 | Hires the **Warlord** titan instantly |

**Watchpost vs Gun Bastion:** the Watchpost is your cheap, air-droppable picket — use it to
grab Wellsprings, contest Obelisks and throw up vision/defense anywhere on the map. The Gun
Bastion is the heavy anchor you build *at* a position you intend to hold.

## 3. The mercenaries (units)

Everything is **instant** (≈0.5s "build" time). Cost is the only gate.

**Core four (from the Haven):**
| Unit | Cost | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Enforcer** | 90 | Cheap rifle merc. Your bulk body and the unit your Reinforcement Drop deploys. |
| **Arbalest** | 160 | Long-range beam marksman (225 range). Glass cannon — keep it screened. Needs a Countinghouse. |
| **Juggernaut** | 320 | Heavy splashing shell bruiser (320 HP). Anti-clump/anti-armour. Needs a Countinghouse. |
| **Marauder** | 150 | Short-range grenade bruiser with splash. Solid frontline filler. |

**Specialists (NEW — from the Mercenary Guild):**
| Unit | Cost | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Gun Hand** | 80 | Very fast (140 speed) cheap raider. Scout, run down stragglers, harass enemy economy. |
| **Dragoon** | 175 | Fast aerial rapid-fire gunner. Raids, chases flyers, mops up. |
| **Sawbones** | 120 | Field-medic aura (heals nearby mercs). The glue that makes expensive mercs sustainable. |
| **Ironhide** | 240 | 560-HP armoured brawler. The frontline wall the Syndicate always lacked — soaks fire so your Arbalests/Demolishers do the work. |
| **Demolisher** | 260 | Slow long-range (245) siege shell with huge splash. Your turtle-breaker — out-ranges turrets. Fragile up close; screen it. |

**The new "standard" army:** Ironhides in front → Arbalests/Demolishers behind → Sawbones
attached → Gun Hands/Dragoons flanking to snipe siege and raid. Juggernauts/Marauders fill the
midline. This is a genuine combined-arms force now, not just enforcer spam.

**Warlord (apex):** 1300-HP siege gun + four autocannons, hired instantly for 820. Needs the War
Exchange + Siege Ordnance researched. A game-ender if your treasury can stomach the price.

## 4. Active ability — Reinforcement Drop (NEW)

The Haven can **air-drop a free squad of three Enforcers anywhere on the map** (not within
~300px of an enemy building), on a ~42s reload. Target it from the Haven's command card.

Uses:
- **Defense:** instant bodies on a base that's being hit (the AI does this automatically).
- **Map control:** drop a squad onto a contested Obelisk or beside a Wellspring you're claiming.
- **Harass:** drop near (not on) an enemy expansion to force a response.

It's free value on a timer — fire it every time it's up.

## 5. Opening build order

1. Haven hires **2–3 Enforcers** for safety; scout with one.
2. **Countinghouse** as soon as you can afford it (raises the cap so interest starts working).
3. Push Enforcers/Gun Hands out to the **nearest Obelisk** and claim it — this is your real
   economy starting. Drop a **Watchpost** to help hold it.
4. **Mercenary Guild** (~80s in) to open the specialist roster. **Black Market** for research.
5. Bank toward the cap; spend overflow on **Ironhide + Arbalest/Demolisher** cores.
6. **Bullion Vault(s)** once you have territory and want the treasury compounding harder.
7. Grab more Obelisks/Wellsprings the whole game. Fire Reinforcement Drop on cooldown.

---

## 6. Tech priorities (Black Market)

- **Economy → Logistics I/II** early: more income compounds with your interest engine beautifully.
- **Offense → Weapons I/II** to make your instant army hit above its cost.
- **Siege Ordnance** when you want the **Warlord** apex (and it buffs Demolisher/Juggernaut splash).
- **Defense → Plating** if you're getting raided; pairs with Ironhides for an immovable front.

---

## 7. Strengths, weaknesses, win conditions

**Strengths**
- Instant reinforcement — no production lag. You can rebuild an army the moment gold allows.
- Severance + bounty mean **trading armies favours you**. Fight constantly.
- Compounding treasury can snowball out of control if you hold territory.

**Weaknesses**
- A poor early game if you turtle: the cap stalls and your interest does nothing.
- Spending dumps your interest base, so big purchases briefly *slow* your economy — time them.
- Arbalests/Demolishers are fragile; without Ironhides/Sawbones they evaporate.

**Win conditions**
1. **Territory snowball** — grab Obelisks/Wellsprings early, let interest run wild, drown the
   map in instant mercs.
2. **Bounty aggression** — keep fighting where you have the advantage; every kill funds the next.
3. **Warlord timing** — bank to a fortune, drop a Warlord (or several) the enemy can't match.

**Against you:** opponents will try to deny you territory and force you to turtle (where your
economy stalls). Don't let them — your whole game is on the map. If you're ahead, never stop
spending the overflow; idle gold above the cap earns nothing.
