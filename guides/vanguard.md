# The Vanguard — Faction Guide

> A full Earth war machine: the game's textbook **combined-arms macro** faction. If you've
> played any classic RTS, this is home — workers, barracks, factories, tanks, aircraft.

- **Core:** Headquarters (1600 HP) — trains Workers, crystal drop-off
- **Resource:** Crystal (mined by Workers from finite nodes)
- **Base unit cap:** 54 (the highest "normal" cap; Supply Depots add +6 each)
- **Identity:** No gimmick — just the broadest, most flexible toolbox in the game. Your skill
  ceiling is in macro and army composition.

---

## 1. Economy — finite crystal, so you MUST expand

Workers auto-mine the nearest crystal node and haul it back to the HQ or a **Supply Depot**
(both are drop-offs). The catch: **nodes are finite.** Your starter patch *will* run dry, and
when it does your whole war machine starves. There is no passive crystal minting.

- Keep building Workers early (aim ~12–16 across your active nodes).
- **Supply Depots** are forward drop-offs *and* raise your unit cap (+6). Chain them out toward
  fresh nodes so Workers don't make the long trek home — that's your reach across the map.
- A Depot built **beside a Wellspring** harnesses it for a big crystal flood.
- Capture **Obelisks** for +3.4/s uncapped income — vital once nodes thin out.

**The throughline:** the Vanguard is constantly pushing Workers and Depots outward to claim
new ground. Sitting still = slow death.

---

## 2. Build order

1. HQ → pump **Workers** (don't stop at 4 — keep going).
2. **Barracks** → Marines for early safety and scouting.
3. **Supply Depot** toward your second node; start eyeing the nearest Obelisk.
4. **Factory** (needs Barracks) → Outriders to raid, then Tanks.
5. **Tech Lab** for upgrades; **Airfield** (needs Factory) for Gunships/Bombers.
6. Turrets/Pillboxes to hold chokes. Expand relentlessly.

---

## 3. Units

**Barracks:** Marine (60, backbone rifle) · Rocketeer (85, anti-armour/air, out-ranges Marines)
· Sniper (110, fragile long-range burst) · Medic (75, healing aura).

**Factory:** Outrider (90, fast recon/raider) · Siege Tank (200, splash anti-armour) · Hellhound
(170, short-range flame, melts clumped infantry) · Goliath (270, durable long-range walker) ·
Artillery (250, huge range + splash, fragile — needs Tech Lab) · **Landship** (520, 900 HP —
a colossal broadside landship whose guns run the length of the hull. Wheels to bring a
target abeam, but never has to stop moving to do it; massive splash once lined up).

**Airfield:** Gunship (180, fast rapid-fire raider) · Vulture Bomber (240, heavy splash payload).

**Apex:** **Leviathan** (700) — walking dreadnought, siege cannon + four autocannons. Built at
the **Dominion Yard** (needs Airfield + Tech Lab + Siege Ordnance).

**Composition is everything.** Marines + Rocketeers + Medics is a fine all-purpose ball; add
Tanks/Goliaths for armour, Artillery to break turtles, Gunships to snipe siege and raid. No
single unit wins — the *mix* does.

---

## 4. Tech priorities (Tech Lab)
- **Economy → Logistics** early to stretch finite crystal.
- **Offense → Weapons I/II** — your bread-and-butter army scales hard with these.
- **Siege Ordnance** for Artillery/Tank splash and the Leviathan.
- **Supply Lines / War Economy** for cap so you can field the big combined-arms ball.

---

## 5. Strengths, weaknesses, win conditions
**Strengths:** unmatched flexibility; an answer to everything; strong defensive structures
(Pillbox, Turret); high cap.
**Weaknesses:** finite crystal punishes greed and passivity; no economic gimmick to snowball —
you win on execution, not on a free engine.
**Win conditions:** out-macro and out-position. Hold more nodes + Obelisks, keep a perfect
combined-arms ball reinforced, and grind the enemy core with Tanks/Artillery under air cover.
Protect your HQ — and your Workers (enemy raiders love them).
