# Verdant Bloom — Faction Guide

> A living garden that wages war. **Three harvests** feed it, your finest plants and beasts demand
> a *mix* of all three, and your grandest works are titanic and even indestructible.

- **Core:** Heartwood (2100 HP) — musters the army, spawns free Saplings, grows the garden
- **Resources:** Sap (bulk), Pollen (second), Loam (third)
- **Base unit cap:** 84 (very high) + a **separate** free-Sapling cap (30)
- **Identity:** The widest economy and tech in the game. Spread fertile ground; field everything
  from free Sapling swarms to indestructible world-trees.

---

## 1. Economy — three harvests, and the home garden caps out

- **Sap** — your bulk economy, from mature **Blooms** (`1.2 + 1.6 per Bloom`), but it **plateaus at
  6 Blooms**. A home garden caps out.
- **Pollen** — from **Pollen Spires** (0.9/s). Scarce; demanded by finer plants/beasts.
- **Loam** — from **Mulch Beds** (0.7/s). Scarce; feeds great trees and roots your Thornwalls.

The scarce Pollen and Loam are choked at home. **The answer is to spread:** plant a **Bloom on a
Wellspring** to root a thriving new ecosystem that pours out bonus **Sap + Pollen + Loam** *and*
grows a fertilising buff (nearby plants/beasts hit harder). Claim fertile ground across the map —
your diverse late-game army *demands* it. Capture **Obelisks** too (+3.4/s).

---

## 2. The free Sapling swarm
The Heartwood and **Groves** spawn free **Saplings** forever, on their own cap (30, separate from
your main army cap — so chaff never crowds out your real units). **Heartwood Saplings** are
expansions that spread your build range far and trickle their own Saplings.

---

## 3. Tech: the Heartwood, Grove, and Arboretum

Production is split across buildings so no command card overflows:
- **Heartwood** → the whole army (Thornling, Treant, Ancient, + Bramblehorn/Spore Caller which
  need an Arboretum) + core economy (Bloom, Pollen Spire, Mulch Bed, Grove, Arboretum).
- **Grove** → free Saplings + defenses (Bramble, Thornwall, Spore Vent, Spore Blossom, Fertiliser
  Pod) + Great Root + Heartwood Sapling.
- **Arboretum** (research lab) → the three **Grafts**, the **Erdtree**, and the apex **Heart Grove**.

### Heartwood Grafts (faction-wide mutations — root beside the Heartwood)
- **Necrotic Graft:** your plants/beasts feed on enemy dead nearby (permanent stacking buff), and
  your buildings no longer die — they collapse into **withered husks** you nurse back with Fertiliser Pods.
- **Moonsign Graft:** reveals the whole map and **hastens everything** (move/attack/growth).
- **Wildgrowth Graft:** Saplings ignore the cap and erupt as bigger, fiercer **mutated** Saplings.

### Indestructible works
- **Erdtree** — one colossal **invulnerable** world-tree that shells all who near it; raises
  **Erdtree Walls** (cheap, indestructible, impassable — chain them into ramparts).
- **Great Root / Thornwall** — titanic living walls to funnel armies.

**Apex:** **Eldertree** (700) from the **Heart Grove** — a titanic world-tree, splashing blows, an
aura that heals the garden. Needs Grove + Arboretum + Siege Ordnance.

---

## 4. Build order
1. Heartwood → free Saplings cover you early. Plant **2–3 Blooms** for Sap.
2. **Grove** for more free Saplings + defenses; **Pollen Spire + Mulch Bed** to open the second/third harvests.
3. Expand: plant a **Bloom on a Wellspring** to root a new ecosystem (this is your real scaling).
4. **Arboretum** → pick a **Graft** that fits your plan; tech upgrades.
5. Field Treants/Ancients → Bramblehorns/Spore Callers; wall key ground with Thornwalls/Erdtree.
6. Heart Grove + Eldertree to close.

---

## 5. Tech priorities (Arboretum)
- **Economy → Logistics** to ease the Pollen/Loam squeeze.
- **Offense → Weapons** — multiplies across your big unit count and Saplings.
- **Siege Ordnance** for Spore Caller/Ancient/Eldertree splash.
- Pick the **Graft** that matches your game: Necrotic (grind/sustain), Moonsign (tempo/vision),
  Wildgrowth (swarm).

---

## 6. Strengths, weaknesses, win conditions
**Strengths:** enormous, diverse toolbox; free swarm on a separate cap; indestructible walls and
world-trees; powerful map-wide Graft mutations; very high cap.
**Weaknesses:** three resources to juggle; Pollen/Loam are scarce without map control; the home
garden caps, so passive play stalls; slower, deliberate units.
**Win conditions:** claim fertile ground everywhere (fonts = bonus harvests + buffs), tech wide,
and grind with a buffed garden behind indestructible walls — then crack their core with Spore
Callers/Eldertree. Protect the Heartwood.
