# Warden Covenant — Faction Guide

> A walled, secretive brotherhood. **The only self-sufficient faction:** alone among the powers
> you needn't march out for the map — your standing buildings *are* your economy. Wall up,
> turret up, grind forward. The deepest tech tree in the game.

- **Core:** Bastion Keep (2400 HP) — trains Tier-1 foot, grows Tier-1/tech buildings
- **Resources:** Stone (primary), Iron (Tier 2+), Powder (grand projects)
- **Base unit cap:** 60
- **Identity:** Turtle and tech. Slow, armoured, unstoppable. You can win from a corner — the
  only faction that can.

---

## 1. Economy — buildings ARE income

Your Stone income scales with the **total HP of your standing buildings** plus a trickle per
Stone Quarry: `1.0 + 0.0011 × (building HP) + 1.8 × quarries`. So **every wall, tower and hall
you raise literally pays you.** Ramparts (1100 HP, dirt cheap) are fantastic value — they wall
you in *and* fatten your income.

- **You cannot harness Wellsprings** — and you don't need to. Ignore them.
- **Three resources:**
  - **Stone** — everything Tier 1; minted by Quarries + building mass.
  - **Iron** — Tier 2+; minted **only by Iron Forges** (1.0/s each). Build many.
  - **Powder** — the grandest projects only; a trickle from Powder Mills, but the real haul is
    on the map: every **Obelisk** you hold trickles Powder, and **Munitions Bunkers** drop 90
    Powder when cracked. The doomsday tech is the one thing that pulls the Warden outdoors.

---

## 2. The distributed tech tree

Advanced structures are raised from **other advanced structures**, not all from the Keep — this
is the Warden's signature. The chain:

- **Keep** → Tier-1 foot (Sentinel, Warden Guard, Pikeman) + Ramparts, Bastions, Quarries, Forges,
  Powder Mills, Oil Cauldrons, **War Foundry**, **War College**.
- **War Foundry** (needs Forge) → Bombards/Ironclads; grows **Redoubt** + **Grand Arsenal**.
- **War College** (needs Bastion) → research lab; grows **Bunker**, **Ballista Tower**, **Hall of Oaths**.
- **Hall of Oaths** → Halberdiers, Marshals (banner officer, heal aura).
- **Grand Arsenal** (needs Foundry + College) → Castellan/Trebuchet; grows **The Bulwark** + **The Worldbreaker**.

---

## 3. Units & defenses

**Foot:** Sentinel (80, tough melee line) · Warden Guard (120, armoured ranged) · Pikeman (110+10 Iron, anti-armour).
**Tier 2:** Bombard (siege artillery) · Ironclad (560-HP melee bruiser) · Halberdier (anti-armour cleaver) · Marshal (heal/repair aura).
**Tier 3:** **Castellan** (940-HP siege colossus) · **Trebuchet** (longest range in the game, huge splash, fragile).
**Towers:** Bastion · Oil Cauldron (burning splash) · Bunker (1400 HP) · Ballista Tower (305 range single-bolt) · Redoubt (long-range splash).

**Apex line (Warden-specific):**
- **The Bulwark** (citadel) — 7200-HP doomsday fortress: huge siege cannon + four machine-guns.
- **The Worldbreaker** — 9000 HP, longest passive range in the game, **+ the active GUSTAV STRIKE:
  nuke any point across most of the map** (~1-min reload). The single most expensive thing anyone
  can build (17000 Stone / 4250 Iron / 900 Powder). Needs Arsenal + Bulwark + Siege Ordnance.

---

## 4. Build order
1. Keep → **Sentinels** for safety. **Quarry** for Stone.
2. **Ramparts** liberally — cheap HP that walls *and* pays. **Bastions** on the wall.
3. **Iron Forges** (2+) to open Tier 2. **Powder Mill** if going for grand projects.
4. **War College** (research + Bunker/Ballista/Hall) and **War Foundry** (Bombard/Ironclad/Redoubt).
5. Grind out with heavy troops + siege; wall forward toward the enemy.
6. Tech to **Grand Arsenal** → Castellan/Trebuchet → Bulwark → (optionally) Worldbreaker.

---

## 5. Tech priorities (War College)
- **Defense → Plating/Fortification** synergises with your whole identity (tankier walls = more income too, indirectly).
- **Offense → Weapons** for the siege line; **Siege Ordnance** is Weapons-II-gated and unlocks the apex path.
- **Economy → Logistics** if you want to tech faster; you're rarely income-starved on Stone.

---

## 6. Strengths, weaknesses, win conditions
**Strengths:** self-sufficient (win from a corner); deepest tech; the toughest buildings and best
static defense; map-spanning siege (Trebuchet, Worldbreaker).
**Weaknesses:** **slow** — early aggression can catch you before you've walled; Iron-gated mid-game;
must venture out for Powder if you want the doomsday tech; immobile by nature.
**Win conditions:** turtle into an impregnable fortress, out-tech everyone, and grind forward
behind walls with siege — or simply Gustav-Strike their core into dust from across the map. Hold the Keep.
