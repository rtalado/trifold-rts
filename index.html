THE VANGUARD

Earth's Army
  • Textbook combined arms. The widest, most flexible roster in the war — an answer to everything.
  • Infantry (marines, rocketeers, snipers, medics), armour (outriders, tanks, hellhounds, goliaths, artillery) and air (gunships, bombers).
  • Crystal nodes are finite — once your starter patch runs dry you must push Workers out to claim fresh nodes. Supply Depots are forward drop-offs that make far nodes worth mining.
  • Turrets & pillboxes dig in; a base that grows into a fortress.
  • Lose your Headquarters and the campaign collapses.
COMMAND THE VANGUARD

MYRIAD SWARM

The Flood
  • No workers. No mining. Your creep IS your economy — but a home creep-blob soon maxes out.
  • To grow you must spread OUTWARD and blanket Wellsprings in creep — each font you cover floods you with Biomass.
  • Units cost nothing: growths breed them endlessly, for free. Steer the tide with a rally point.
  • Corruption: every attack rots & weakens the foe; corrupted enemies that die burst free Larva. Hire corrosive elites and cast the Hive's Corruption Bloom to infect a whole army.
  • The swarm heals on creep. Off creep, it is merely meat.
  • Protect the Hive at all costs.
BECOME THE FLOOD

SOLARI EXODUS

The Pilgrims
  • No base. No buildings. Ever.
  • One sacred Ark — mobile fortress, factory and treasury in one. Deploy it on crystal to siphon energy.
  • A handful of priceless, shielded elites: blinking seekers, beam lancers, aegis guardians.
  • Ascend the Ark through eight tiers — pour a fortune into it to forge a roaming superweapon with a map-scorching Solar Lance.
  • Shields regenerate — retreat, recover, strike again. Lose nothing, or lose everything.
LEAD THE EXODUS

ASHEN CHOIR

The Revenants
  • All death feeds you. Every unit that falls — yours or theirs — pays Essence.
  • REANIMATION: a share of every unit that dies rises as a free Husk for you. Toll the Ossuary's Dirge to reap a whole field.
  • The lattice sustains your spirits; in the field they fade — but feast on the damage they deal.
  • Raise a Necropolis for the elite undead; crawl the lattice out to a Wellspring for a surge of Essence.
  • Silence the Ossuary and the Choir is no more.
RAISE THE CHOIR

GILDED SYNDICATE

The Magnates
  • Gold breeds gold — but only as far as your TERRITORY allows. Interest compounds up to a cap set by the ground you hold.
  • A bank in a corner stalls; capture Obelisks and harness Wellsprings (park a Watchpost beside one) to lift the cap and let the fortune snowball.
  • Mercenaries arrive instantly; every kill pays a bounty; Watchposts air-drop across the map (not into enemy territory).
  • The Haven is vault and fortress in one — bankrupt is dead.
FUND THE WAR

WARDEN COVENANT

The Bulwark
  • Two resources. Stone from building mass (+ Quarries); Iron from Forges.
  • Distributed tech: build advanced structures from advanced ones — College → Foundry → Arsenal → Bulwark.
  • Slow, heavily armoured units that grind forward and never break.
  • Self-sufficient. Alone among the powers, you needn't march out for the map — no scrabbling for Wellsprings, just wall up and grind. A secretive brotherhood.
  • Hold the Bastion Keep at the heart of the hold.
RAISE THE BULWARK

EMBER NOMADS

The Warband
  • No mines. You fund the war by waging it. Every point of damage you deal pays Plunder.
  • BURNING: every attack ignites the foe — and burn damage keeps paying Plunder as they cook.
  • Free Emberlings, a fire-breathing Cinder Drake and the Molten Magmaur — call the Firestorm to raze a whole army.
  • Keep attacking or starve. Throw forward War Camps beside Wellsprings for extra Plunder.
  • Guard the War Pyre.
LIGHT THE WARBAND

VERDANT BLOOM

The Grove
  • A slow, unstoppable garden on THREE harvests — Sap, Pollen, Loam. But a home garden caps out, and Pollen & Loam stay choked.
  • Plant a Bloom on a Wellspring to root a thriving new ecosystem: it pours out bonus Sap, Pollen and Loam, and buffs nearby plants & beasts. Spread to fertile ground all over the map.
  • Groves breed free Saplings, forever. A tide of green.
  • Patient and weak early; overwhelming late.
  • Protect the Heartwood.
SOW THE GROVE

STORMFORGE DYNASTY

The Engine
  • An engine that accelerates — by holding ground, not idling. Home Dynamos pay a flat rate and soon max out.
  • The acceleration lives on the map: raise a Dynamo on a Wellspring (a Storm Font) and the longer you hold it, the more Power it ramps out.
  • Few but devastating, shielded machines. Seize fonts early and never let go.
  • Defend the Storm Reactor.
SPARK THE ENGINE

OBSIDIAN PACT

The Martyrs
  • Death is your harvest — your own. Every unit you lose spills Blood to fund the next, greater summoning.
  • BLOOD FRENZY: each unit that dies whips the horde around it into a rage — harder, faster blows.
  • Throw cheap Thralls & Flayers into the grinder; raise the Flesh Vats for a healer, air and heavy horrors.
  • Work the Altar's Crimson Rite to rupture a field and enrage the swarm. Bone Shrines on Wellsprings for extra Blood.
  • Keep the Blood Altar.
SEAL THE PACT

THE VIRULENT STRAIN

The Adaptation
  • Dirt-cheap, weak spam. Spawnlings cost almost nothing — losing them is the plan.
  • ADAPTATION: survive enough of one damage TYPE (bullet, beam, glob, shell, melee) and a unit hardens a strong resistance to it — but earning a new resistance always burns away the old one.
  • Getting hit pays you. Every point of damage your units endure earns Genome — throwing chaff into the grinder funds the next wave.
  • Ranged Stingers & beam Lashers, a tanky Brute, siege Bloater, flying Drifter and a Mender; free Whelps from a Spawning Well.
  • Trigger the Progenitor's ADAPTIVE SURGE to instantly harden your whole army against everything at once.
  • Protect the Progenitor.
EMBRACE THE STRAIN
