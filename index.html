IRON VANGUARD

The Architects
  • Classic macro RTS. Workers harvest crystal, you build the war machine.
  • Barracks, factories, defensive turrets — a base that grows into a fortress.
  • Balanced units: cheap marines, long-range snipers, siege tanks.
  • Lose your Headquarters and the campaign collapses.
COMMAND THE VANGUARD

MYRIAD SWARM

The Flood
  • No workers. No mining. Your creep IS your economy — every covered tile feeds you biomass.
  • Units cost nothing: growths breed them endlessly, for free.
  • You don't command soldiers, you steer a tide. Set the swarm rally and spread.
  • The swarm heals on creep. Off creep, it is merely meat.
  • Protect the Hive at all costs.
BECOME THE FLOOD

SOLARI EXODUS

The Pilgrims
  • No base. No buildings. Ever.
  • One sacred Ark — mobile fortress, factory and treasury in one. Deploy it on crystal to siphon energy.
  • A handful of priceless, shielded elites: blinking seekers, beam lancers, aegis guardians.
  • Shields regenerate — retreat, recover, strike again. Lose nothing, or lose everything.
LEAD THE EXODUS

ASHEN CHOIR

The Revenants
  • All death feeds you. Every unit that falls — yours or theirs — pays Essence.
  • The lattice sustains your spirits; in the field they fade — but feast on the damage they deal.
  • Build only within the lattice: Soul Conduits extend your reach and trickle Essence.
  • Silence the Ossuary and the Choir is no more.
RAISE THE CHOIR

GILDED SYNDICATE

The Magnates
  • Gold breeds gold. Your treasury earns compound interest — hoard or hire.
  • Mercenaries arrive instantly, for a price. Every kill pays a bounty.
  • Air-drop Watchposts across the map (not into enemy territory); Countinghouses raise the interest cap.
  • The Haven is vault and fortress in one — bankrupt is dead.
FUND THE WAR

WARDEN COVENANT

The Bulwark
  • Two resources. Stone from building mass (+ Quarries); Iron from Forges.
  • Distributed tech: build advanced structures from advanced ones — College → Foundry → Arsenal → Bulwark.
  • Slow, heavily armoured units that grind forward and never break.
  • Hold the Bastion Keep at the heart of the hold.
RAISE THE BULWARK

EMBER NOMADS

The Warband
  • No mines. You fund the war by waging it. Every point of damage you deal pays Plunder.
  • Fast, dirt-cheap, fragile raiders — strike fast and never stop moving.
  • Keep attacking or starve: idleness is death.
  • Guard the War Pyre.
LIGHT THE WARBAND

VERDANT BLOOM

The Grove
  • A slow, unstoppable garden. Each mature Bloom pays Sap — the more you grow, the faster you snowball.
  • Groves breed free Saplings, forever. A tide of green.
  • Patient and weak early; overwhelming late.
  • Protect the Heartwood.
SOW THE GROVE

STORMFORGE DYNASTY

The Engine
  • An engine that only accelerates. Your income ramps the longer the game runs — time is your weapon.
  • Dynamos supercharge the climb; few but devastating machines.
  • Survive the early game and become inevitable.
  • Defend the Storm Reactor.
SPARK THE ENGINE

OBSIDIAN PACT

The Martyrs
  • Death is your harvest — your own. Every unit you lose spills Blood to fund the next, greater summoning.
  • Throw cheap Thralls into the grinder; raise Behemoths from their deaths.
  • Reckless by design — sacrifice is strategy.
  • Keep the Blood Altar.
SEAL THE PACT
